Sereus Chat

Private messaging — no accounts, no servers.

Chat directly with people you trust, by invitation only. No phone number, no sign-up, no company in the middle. Your conversations live on your own devices and move over the Sereus peer-to-peer fabric.

How it works

  1. Install the app — the only thing you enter is a display name.
  2. Invite a friend with a QR code or a link; accepting it forms a private strand between you.
  3. Chat directly — messages sync across your own devices and your contacts', not through a server.
  4. Grow your cadre — add your own always-on device (a drone or server) so you're reachable even when your phone is asleep.

No accounts and no central servers means no spam lists to end up on and no provider that can read your messages. Trust is established by invitation and held by cryptographic keys you control.

Got an invitation?

If someone sent you a Sereus Chat invite link or QR code, install the app first, then open the link (or scan the code in the app) to accept it and start chatting.
